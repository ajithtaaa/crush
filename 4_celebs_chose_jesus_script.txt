4 CELEBRITIES WHO CHOSE FAITH OVER FAME
ElevenLabs voiceover script (original wording, copy everything below the line)
--------------------------------------------------------------

[slowly] One of them turned down ten million dollars. Another was struck by lightning on set.
These 4 stars risked everything for their faith.

Number 4. [slowly] Chris Tucker.
Rush Hour made him a superstar. The studio wanted him back for Next Friday, and the paycheck was massive.
His answer? No.
He said he didn't want to curse on screen anymore, and he wasn't going to change his beliefs for money.
Millions of dollars, gone. And he says he has no regrets.

Number 3. [slowly] Jim Caviezel.
In 2004, he played Jesus in The Passion of the Christ. People warned him, this role will end your career.
He said yes anyway.
During filming, he dislocated his shoulder, got hypothermia, and was hit by lightning.
The movie made over six hundred million dollars. But Caviezel says Hollywood stopped calling him.

Number 2. [slowly] Kirk Cameron.
In the eighties, he was one of the biggest teen idols in America, on the hit show Growing Pains.
Then, at seventeen, he became a Christian, and everything changed.
He started refusing scenes he felt crossed the line, even asking writers to change scripts.
He left Hollywood's spotlight behind, and now makes faith films with his own family.

And number one. [slowly] Kanye West.
He was one of the most famous rappers alive. Then, in 2019, he stunned the world.
He started holding church every Sunday, with a full gospel choir.
He released an album called Jesus Is King, with no swearing at all.
Some called it a real change, others called it a phase. But nobody saw it coming.
