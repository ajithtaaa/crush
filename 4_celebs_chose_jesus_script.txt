4 CELEBRITIES WHO CHOSE JESUS OVER FAME

--------------------------------------------------------------

[slowly] 4 celebrities who chose Jesus over fame, and paid the price for it.

Number 4. [slowly] Chris Tucker.
At the height of his career, he was one of the highest-paid actors in Hollywood.
But he shocked everyone by walking away from a huge payday for Next Friday.
He said he no longer felt comfortable with the cursing and certain scenes.
Tucker chose to follow his faith, even if it meant leaving millions behind.

Number 3. [slowly] Jim Caviezel.
He didn't just play Jesus in The Passion of the Christ. He fully embraced the role.
He was warned that it could hurt his Hollywood career, but he took it anyway.
While filming, he was even struck by lightning.
After the movie, he says the big roles stopped coming, but he stood firm in his faith.

Number 2. [slowly] Kirk Cameron.
He was once a teen heartthrob on Growing Pains.
After finding faith, he started turning down certain scenes, including on-screen kisses.
His choices frustrated producers, but he didn't back down.
He later stepped away from mainstream fame to focus on faith-based films.

And number one. [slowly] Kanye West.
His transformation surprised the entire world.
Known for bold and controversial music, he shifted to faith-driven work and launched Sunday Service.
He even changed his music, focusing on gospel with his album, Jesus Is King.
Love it or question it, his move from rap icon to faith-focused artist became a cultural moment.
