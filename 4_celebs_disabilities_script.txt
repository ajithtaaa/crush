4 CELEBRITIES WHO HAVE DISABILITIES
ElevenLabs voiceover script (copy everything below the line)
--------------------------------------------------------------

4 celebrities who have disabilities. And number one needed a kidney from her best friend.

Number 4. Millie Bobby Brown.
The Stranger Things star was born with partial hearing loss in one ear.
Over time, she lost the hearing in that ear completely.
She played Eleven while being deaf in one ear, and most fans had no idea.
Today, she even uses sign language and speaks openly about it.

Number 3. Ariana Grande.
In 2017, a bomb went off after her concert in Manchester.
Twenty-two people lost their lives.
Ariana later revealed she was living with post-traumatic stress disorder.
She even shared a brain scan to show how deeply the trauma affected her.

Number 2. Justin Bieber.
In 2022, he suddenly couldn't move half of his face.
He was diagnosed with Ramsay Hunt syndrome, a rare condition that causes facial paralysis.
He couldn't blink one eye, or smile on one side.
He had to cancel his world tour to recover.

And number one, Selena Gomez.
In 2015, she revealed she had lupus, a disease where the body attacks itself.
The illness damaged her kidneys so badly that she needed a transplant.
Her best friend, Francia Raísa, gave her one of her own kidneys.
Today, Selena uses her story to help others facing the same battle.
